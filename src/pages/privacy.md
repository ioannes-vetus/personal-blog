# Privacy

_Last updated: October 13, 2026_

This is a personal website with a blog. It has no accounts, no comments, no newsletter and no
contact forms, and it does not use cookies. This page explains the little data that is processed
when you visit it.

## Who is responsible

Ján Starý, reachable at [jan@janstary.com](mailto:jan@janstary.com).

## What is processed

### Hosting

The site is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/). When you visit it, GitHub logs
your IP address for security purposes, as described in the
[GitHub Pages documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/about-github-pages#data-collection)
and the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
I have no access to these logs.

### Analytics

Visits are counted with [Umami Cloud](https://umami.is/), a privacy-focused analytics service.
Umami does not use cookies, does not store IP addresses and does not track you across websites.
It records anonymous, aggregated information: the page visited, the referring site, browser,
operating system, device type and country. When you open a result from the site search, the
search query and the chosen page are recorded as an anonymous event. See the
[Umami FAQ](https://docs.umami.is/docs/faq) for details.

Analytics only run on janstary.com itself.

### Stored in your browser

Your light/dark theme choice is saved in your browser's local storage so it is remembered on your
next visit. It never leaves your device.

### Everything else

Fonts, images and the search index are served from this site, with no requests to third parties.
Site search and the CV download run entirely in your browser. Links to LinkedIn and GitHub take
you to those services, whose own privacy policies then apply.

## Legal basis and retention

Processing is based on legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in running the site securely and
understanding how it is used. Retention is determined by GitHub and Umami according to their
policies linked above.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or
object to its processing, and to data portability. To exercise any of them, email me at the
address above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Slovakia, that is
the [Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic](https://dataprotection.gov.sk/).

## Changes

If the way this site handles data changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top
revised.
